**BlackPDF** is a free and open PDF viewer and editor that works offline: your documents never leave your computer.
Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese and French.

### Downloads

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 (1903+) / 11 | `BlackPDF-<version>-Setup.exe` |
| Linux (glibc 2.35+) | `BlackPDF-<version>-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint | `blackpdf_<version>_amd64.deb` |

The Windows installer is not code-signed yet: if SmartScreen shows a warning, click **More info** and **Run anyway**.
`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the checksums of every file.

### Highlights

- Viewer with tabs, thumbnails, outline, search, night mode and split view.
- Organize, merge, split, alternate, extract, rotate, compress and convert PDFs.
- Annotations and threaded comments compatible with Acrobat; redaction.
- Digital signatures with your certificate (PAdES) and signature verification.
- Built-in OCR in four languages.
- 3D viewer for U3D and PRC models embedded in PDFs.
